350Z vehicle audio – version 0.5.0

The following original recordings were extracted from the locally supplied
NFS Underground 2 CD images:

Acceleration:
SOUND/ENGINE/GIN_350Z_Jason_shrt2.gin
Lifting off:
SOUND/ENGINE/GIN_Nissan_350ZX_Jason_DCL.gin
Road noise:
SOUND/IG_GLOBAL/ROADNOISE_00_MB.abk, substream 4
Tyre skid:
SOUND/SKIDS/SKID_PAV_MB.abk, substream 1

Decoded with the unmodified official vgmstream CLI r2117:
https://github.com/vgmstream/vgmstream/releases/tag/r2117
GIN format parser:
https://github.com/vgmstream/vgmstream/blob/master/src/meta/gin.c

For engine playback, the decoded recordings were split into eight loops using
the RPM/cycle markers in the GIN files. The loops were time-adjusted, crossfaded
at their boundaries, had their DC offset removed and were volume-matched.
Acceleration and lifting off use separate recordings. The two adjacent RPM
ranges are smoothly mixed.

The horn is a newly synthesized dual-tone horn at 420/500 Hz, not a recording
extracted from NFS.

Reverse gear since 0.5.0:
Newly synthesized periodic loop with a 320 Hz fundamental, harmonics and slight
modulation. Pitch/volume follow reverse speed. It stays silent while stopped,
driving forward and after exiting. The local SOUND file inventory and examined
CSI events have not identified a reverse-gear whine; this is explicitly not an
NFS sample.

Burnout since 0.5.0:
Uses the existing original SKID_PAV_MB.abk sample (substream 1). During a burnout,
the previous minimum driving speed requirement for skid audio is removed; rear
wheel RPM and ground contact instead determine its volume. The engine stays
audibly under load in first gear at a minimum of 5000 RPM. Smoke uses the original
SFX_SMOKE_B_CS texture with a custom Unity animation.

Turbo:
SOUND/TURBO/TURBO_MED_2_MB.abk, substreams 1 (spooling),
2/3 (two blow-off variants). The role mapping is provisional; the triggers use
custom NFVU2 logic with load/RPM buildup and a single release. The original tables
have separate spooling, low/high-boost blow-off and 0.5 s ChargeTime. Rachel's
actual performance stage remains to be checked.
https://github.com/nlgxzef/NFSU2CarSoundTuner
The original bank checksum and decoder provenance are recorded in the locally generated manifest.

Nitro and purge:
SOUND/NOS/Nitrous_00_MB.abk, loops 1 and 4. Both are original mono-decoded loop
substreams; their boost/purge mapping is provisional and can be adjusted during
import. The local UG2_MAIN_AEMS.csi lists FX_NITROUS and FX_PURGE separately.
tools/prepare_effects_audio.py uses the original loop boundaries, removes DC
offset and matches volume. Checksums, decoder and provenance data are in
.local/audio-effects/manifest.json.

Backfire research:
UG2_ENGINES_AEMS2.csi lists Sputter_Message, CAR_SputOutput and CAR_Sputter.
The reconstructed 350Z sweetener table uses SWTN_CAR_27_MB.abk with SputterVol.
Unlimiter distinguishes CARFX_NITRO, CARFX_EXHAUST_BLOWOFF and CARFX_NOS_BLOWOFF.
This establishes the effect types, not the exact throttle-change/tuning
conditions. The matching sputter substream has not been identified, so no
backfire.wav was exported. The optional flame burst therefore has no bang sound
for now.

https://github.com/nlgxzef/NFSU2CarSoundTuner/blob/master/CarSoundData/SweetnerDataSet/27.ini
https://github.com/vgmstream/vgmstream/blob/master/src/meta/ea_schl_abk.c

Radio:
SDATA/SDAT.VIV contains pfdata/MusicSFx.mpf and MusicSFx.mus.
vgmstream decodes the streams as stereo PCM. Since 0.2.1, the playlist contains
27 verified EA Trax songs with artist and title. The mapping follows the original
song table and MPF events/playback nodes; the title strings were also checked in
the local original EXE (without running it). tools/music_titles.json contains
sources, the MPF hash and SHA-256 values for the decoded WAVs. Incorrect or
modified audio content is not automatically assigned these names. The other 16
previously imported streams are not among the 27 EA Trax events; they remain
local but outside the playlist.
https://github.com/nlgxzef/NFSU2TraxRenamer
https://github.com/RadeonChan/XtraTrax/blob/main/patcher.py
https://github.com/vgmstream/vgmstream/blob/master/src/meta/ea_schl_map_mpf_mus.c

The mod uses its own RPM/shift logic for audio. It does not run the original NFS
driving physics or audio engine. The exact mapping to Rachel's tuning sound has
not been established. The original recordings were selected based on their 350Z
filenames.

The file 350Z-Soundvorschau.wav demonstrates an externally rendered drive:
idling, acceleration with gear shifts, lifting off, horn and fade-out. It is not
a recording from the game.

RUST TURRET (0.9.0, optional)
From the user's Rust installation: ak47-gunshot-body-01 through -03,
ak47-gunshot-tail-outdoor-01, autoturret-movement-loop,
autoturret-reload-full and autoturret-turn-on.
UnityPy exports the original samples; spatial sources are downmixed to mono.
Firing cadence, reload time and volumes are NFVU2 adjustments. Since 0.10.0,
`explosive-ammo-close` from Rust is also used for explosive hits.
HUMMER H2 (0.12.0)
Original: SOUND/ENGINE/GIN_Hummer.gin from the user's own NFSU2 CDs.
Stock mapping: HUMMER -> CarDataMapping 14 -> EngineData 38,
UseDualGinsu=0, 1000–4782 RPM. Eight local PCM loops for Unity;
lifting off uses the same sound at lower load/volume. No separate Hummer
acceleration recording is claimed. Four audible gears and shift points are
adjusted for Valheim. Tyres/horn/reverse whine share the existing system. H2
engine loops are installed externally in audio/h2; original files remain in
ignored .local.

NFS MENU (0.13.0)
Original bank: SOUND/GLOBAL/FE_COMMON_MB.abk from the user's own NFSU2 files.
tools/export_menu.py decodes three short mono samples with vgmstream:
Navigation = Substream 1, Confirm = 3, Back = 2 (configurable CLI values).
Sample provenance and checksums are recorded in menu/provenance.json.
Mapping them to these mod actions is not a verified recreation of the original
event table. There is no synthesized substitute; without media, the menu remains
silent but usable. It uses its own 2D source in the SFX mixer, Menu.SoundVolume
0–1. Icons come from FRONTEND/FrontB.lzc; seven German titles are mapped by hash
from LANGUAGES/Labels.bin and German.bin. Layout and actions are specific to NFVU2.
