NFVU2 0.22.0 – 350Z and Hummer H2, local test for Valheim

NEW: TURBO GAUGE, SOUND AND SMALL TREES
The small left gauge on the 350Z now shows turbo boost and vacuum.
Speed remains digital on the speedometer; the large dial shows RPM.
Turbo and blow-off respond to load and built-up boost: less throttle
produces quieter sound. When releasing the throttle, the needle briefly flutters
into the vacuum range. The Hummer has no turbo gauge.
Small trees and bushes can already be driven through at walking speed.
If the car is directly against a small tree, keep accelerating toward it.
Large trees and rocks still require explosive ammunition.
For the sound test, accelerate first with little, then with more throttle, and release each time.
Also try slowly releasing the controller trigger.
The pressure/volume curve is a custom recreation based on NFS data.

NEW: MENU, DRIVETRAIN AND EXHAUST
When no car is reachable, the menu only shows Garage and Settings.
Up/down on the stick/D-pad selects categories; right or A/Cross switches
to the contents. There, directions navigate between controls.
Left at the start of a row returns to the sidebar. LB/L1 jumps directly
to the sidebar, RB/R1 to the contents. For sliders and selection fields,
press A/Cross first, then change the value with the directions; A or B ends editing.
B/Circle goes back one step at a time. L3/F9 still closes the menu directly.
F9 → Exhaust: choose the Original/Deep/Sport sound variant, nitro flame color, and on the
350Z choose among ten original exhaust tips. STYLE08 is the previous look.
The drivetrain has more torque and stronger launch force forward/reverse.
Normal turret ammunition: 14 piercing damage (three hits at 40 HP without resistance).
Explosive ammunition now also hits large trees, trunks, and mineable rocks.

VEHICLE PROMPTS
In the vehicle, driving controls appear at bottom right with Valheim's original
Xbox/PS5 icons instead of attack/secondary attack. The bindings change in flight and
in the mod menu. The speedometer is above them; on foot, normal Valheim prompts return.
DE/EN and prompt size follow the settings.

CAMERA IN THE MOD MENU
Right stick: freely rotate the camera, including on foot with the menu open.
Mouse: hold right button and drag; in the vehicle, zoom with the mouse wheel.
Release the right mouse button to click or move the menu.
Vehicle and weapon controls remain locked in the menu.

NEW: CONTROLLER AND LANGUAGE
Xbox: RT throttle, LT brake/reverse, left stick steer, right stick camera.
B enter/exit, X nitro, A handbrake, LB turret, R3 horn.
PS5: R2/L2, Circle enter/exit, Square nitro, Cross handbrake, L1 turret.
L3 opens/closes the mod menu. Stick/D-pad navigates, A/Cross confirms,
LB/L1 selects the sidebar, RB/R1 the contents; A starts editing values.
B/Circle goes back or closes the menu without exiting the vehicle.
In flight: RB/R1 up, LB/L1 down. Turret is automatic; X/Square nitro.
B/Circle or F7 in flight: automatic landing, then attempt to exit.
While driving: D-pad left/right songs, up/down driving modes.
Keyboard: 1 off-road, 2 street, 3 drift (350Z only), 4 flight; left/right arrow keys change songs.
F9/L3 → Settings: DE/EN, button display Auto/Xbox/PlayStation,
dead zone, controller camera, invert Y, and driving prompt size.
After menu/pause/focus loss, release held driving keys first.
Controller hardware and menu display still need to be checked in-game.

NEW: HEADLIGHT SHOW
F9 → Lights → enable “Show-off when exiting” (off by default).
After exiting, the headlights flash twice on the left, twice on the
right, then pause. Left/right as seen from the driver's seat. It repeats
until you enter the car or turn off the show. Color and intensity also apply
to the show. Works independently of engine keep-running; saved per vehicle type.
“Reset headlights” also turns the show off.

NEW: METALLIC PAINT
The 350Z and Hummer H2 have more reflective paint with higher gloss.
The change applies to freshly spawned vehicles. Color and vinyl are preserved.

NEW: NITRO TUNNEL VISION AND TAILLIGHT TRAILS
During boost, the image edges blur outward. The car and image center
stay as sharp as possible. The lit taillights leave short red
trails behind them, including in flight. Releasing the control fades the effect out.
F9 → Nitro: toggle tunnel vision and taillight trails separately; effect intensity
from 0 to 100%. Default: both on, 70%. Shared by both cars.
Menu, pause, Alt-Tab, and exiting end the effects immediately.

NEW: EXHAUST NITRO IN FLIGHT
On the 350Z, W + left mouse button or left Ctrl in flight also activates
the normal exhaust flames and nitro sound. The wheel nozzles keep running.
No boost means no exhaust flames; purge and tire smoke remain ground-only.

NEW: BACKGROUND LOADING
F6 or Garage → Spawn: a loading indicator appears at the top with the current
stage. You can keep walking during loading. The finished car appears
in a free spot in front of you; further spawns and entering wait for it.
F8 or “Cancel” ends only the current loading process. Existing
cars remain. Death, teleport, and world changes also cancel it.
Further spawns benefit from the data cache until you leave the world.
Individual texture/graphics steps may still cause brief stutters.

NEW: MOVABLE MENU
Open with F9, grab the title bar with the left mouse button, and drag it
to the desired place. The position is saved. On resolution changes, the menu stays
within the visible screen. The X button still closes it directly.
When no car is reachable, Garage and Settings are visible.

NEW: LOWERING, NFS MENU AND PARKED TURRET
F9 → Wheels: lowering in addition to camber, offset, and rim size.
0–12 cm; 0 restores the stock ride height. More lowering = less ground clearance.
The value is saved per vehicle model. When exiting, the front wheels retain
their steering angle; re-entering resumes steering.

F9 shows categories on the left, original NFS icons, and short navigation sounds.
Switch with the mouse or up/down arrows. Menu volume in the configuration:
Menu → SoundVolume (0 = muted). Import: python tools/export_menu.py.

F9 → Turret: turn on the turret and enter the car once. After exiting,
it automatically guards the area in front of the car (180°, 30 meters).
The laser visibly sweeps if the laser switch is active. Visible
enemies are acquired and shot automatically; reloading remains automatic.
Players and tame animals are not targeted and block the line of fire.
Cover and the car itself also block shots. Automation can be
disabled in the Turret menu and works independently of whether the engine/radio keeps running outside.
Laser color/intensity and selected ammunition apply in both modes.
Menus, focus loss, and player death pause automation. When entering,
aim manually again; release held RMB, then press it again.
H2 still has no roof turret. Unity playtest for 0.13.0 is still pending.

NEW: HUMMER H2 AND GARAGE
F9 → Garage: choose and spawn a Nissan 350Z or Hummer H2.
F6 uses the same selection. Exit before spawning.
Existing cars remain; up to four vehicles at once.
The Garage can also be opened without a car. Find a clear area in front of you.
Go to the desired car: F7 enters the nearest car within 5 m,
F9 shows its settings. F8 removes only that car.
Vehicles are removed on world change and are not saved.

H2: yellow stock body, large 26-inch chrome spinners, and its own SUV sound.
The spinners keep turning after stopping and gradually come to rest.
F9 → Wheels: 22–28 inches, camber, and wheel offset. Tires remain the same size.
Driving mode, lights, neon, wheels, and radio are saved separately for H2/350Z.
Multiple cars of the same model share their settings.
For the H2, engine bay/trunk builds, door animations, nitro, and turret
are not set up yet. The H2 is drivable and its engine runs normally.
When keep-running is enabled, both cars' engines and music can play outside.

NEW: LASER AND SUBWOOFER
F9 → Turret → configure Laser: intensity from 25 to 400 percent, color presets
or a custom RGB color. Default is a stronger beam with a bright core.
The subwoofer cones move automatically to the radio bass.
To see them, open the trunk and start music; they also work outside with
“Keep running when exiting.” They stop when paused/radio is off.

NEW: RUST TURRET
F9 → Turret → enable “Rust AutoTurret mit AK auf dem Dach”.
From the driver's seat, the turret follows the mouse view. Hold the right mouse button:
full auto, 30 rounds, then automatic reload. Unlimited ammunition.
The aiming camera is higher, with the crosshair above the car.
The barrel aims at the world point behind the sight. Orange means:
the barrel is still aligning, is at its elevation limit, or is blocked by the car.
Toggle the Rust laser module in the Turret menu; beam and dot show the barrel path.
Switch ammunition in the same menu between Normal and Explosive; ammo count is at bottom left.
Explosive rounds deal area damage to enemies and dig a small crater on each direct
ground hit. Further hits deepen it up to the digging limit.
Crater locations are saved in the world. Walls and rocks protect the ground
behind them; shots into those obstacles do not dig through them.
Enemies can be hit; solid obstacles stop shots.
The turret does not fire in the menu, when focus is lost, or outside the car.
Afterward, release the right mouse button once before firing again.
Volume under Turret; combines with the vehicle sound volume.
Original Rust models and sounds, with recoil and effects animated for NFVU2.
The new feature still needs an in-game test.

SPEEDOMETER, CAMERA AND DRIFT
F9 → Display: speedometer on/off, eleven NFS designs, color, size, and KM/H or MPH.
Bottom right shows speed, RPM/gear, and NOS level.
RPM and gear are tied to the audible transmission. The nitro gauge follows the tank.
The dynamic camera follows more smoothly in turns; the slider controls how
quickly. Mouse look remains free briefly; while stationary, the view is retained.
Middle mouse button centers the camera. Turning off “Dynamic chase camera” restores
the previous behavior.
F9 → Vehicle → Drift: rear-wheel drive with a loose rear end and more steering angle.
Spacebar applies the rear handbrake; S brakes normally before reversing.
Throttle/countersteering control the drift; tire slip produces tire sounds and smoke.
Switching back to Off-road or Street restores the previous tuning.
Please test the handling and camera follow behavior of this version in-game.
Suspension and controls are based on the confirmed prototype.
The vehicle is temporary and only for local single-player worlds.

CONTROLS
F6: Spawn vehicle.
F7: Enter/exit near a vehicle.
F8: Remove vehicle.
F9: Open/close vehicle menu (key can be changed in the configuration).
W / S: Throttle / brake, then reverse.
Hold W + S: burnout while stationary. While moving, brakes first.
A / D: Steer. Spacebar: brake. Hold H: horn.
Mouse: look around. Mouse wheel: zoom. Middle click: center camera.
Hold left mouse button: nitro while moving forward with throttle; purge while stationary.

VEHICLE MENU
F9 opens the menu at the top left, inside the car or up to five meters away. Off-road mode is
now switched in the menu. The car brakes while navigating; camera
and driving controls are locked, while radio and engine remain audible.
F9 or the cross closes the menu. Chat, inventory, game menu, and
focus loss close the vehicle menu and pause sound.
F9 → Doors: open/close the left/right door, hood, and trunk individually.
Opening is only possible while stationary. Everything closes when driving off or entering.
Door mechanism: Normal, Suicide, or Scissor doors as in NFS. When changing the mechanism,
open doors close first so they do not jump between hinges.
Engine appearance: Stock, Style 1, or Style 2; visual change only.
The engines and animations come from the original files. The existing
interior is very basic; a detailed cockpit is not included.

TRUNK BUILD
F9 → Build: Stock or Build 1/2/3 with two, four, or six mounting slots.
Choose a slot, then its part: cover, Subwoofer 1–4,
Amplifier 1–4, or Screen 1–4. The size matches the selected enclosure.
Build 2 with four subwoofers is installed by default. “Stock System”
restores this system and green static trunk neon.
The LCDs use original static textures, but do not play videos.
Trunk neon can be toggled independently from underbody neon,
set to one of seven colors, and set to static or pulsing.
It lights up when the car is running or the trunk is open. The pulse is a
regular lighting effect, not synchronized to the music beat. Settings are
saved; doors/hood start closed after spawning.
The doors are visually animated; there are no additional door colliders.

RUNNING OVER
Small beeches/firs, ordinary bushes, and hostile creatures can be hit
at a contact speed of about 9 km/h or higher, including in reverse. Damage and
knockback increase with speed; strong enemies may survive.
Valheim handles destruction, impact sounds, effects, and loot.
Large trees, rocks, structures, saplings, players, tame animals, and peaceful
NPCs are excluded. Idling and minor parking bumps cause no
additional vehicle damage. Drivetrain/suspension remain as in 0.5.0.

AFTER EXITING
F9 → Vehicle → “Keep running when exiting” is on by default.
After driving, the engine idles, enabled neon stays on, and
the radio keeps playing from the car at lower volume and with a muffled sound. As you move away,
radio and engine get quieter and are inaudible from about 30 meters.
When doors or trunk open, the music outside gradually gets louder
and clearer; when closed, it gets quieter and more muffled again. The sound comes from
the direction of the open side or trunk; with several openings,
the sound source lies between them. The hood has no effect.
The switch can also be used beside the car and is saved.
Turning it off stops idling/neon and pauses the radio after fading out.
A freshly spawned car stays off until you have entered it once.
The separate radio/neon switches and manual music pause still apply.

WHEELS
F9 → Wheels offers five sliders:
- Front and rear camber, each −15 to +10 degrees. Negative: top tilts inward.
- Front and rear wheel offset, each −10 to +20 cm per wheel. Positive: outward.
- Shared rim size: 16 to 22 inches, based on the NFS 20-inch model.
The tire sidewalls adjust; the outer diameter remains 70 cm.
This setting affects appearance. Suspension and wheelbase are retained.
Brakes follow camber/offset and retain their size.
Values change directly on the car, are saved, and can be reset with
“Reset wheels” to 0 degrees, 0 cm, and 20 inches.
The sliders also work beside the car so you can view the result.

RADIO
Locally imported NFS music starts when entering. The menu provides
track selection, previous/next, pause, and a separate volume control.
When exiting with keep-running enabled, it continues playing muffled and spatially.
Without keep-running, it fades out and pauses; when re-entering, it resumes
at the same position. A manually set pause is preserved.
Valheim music is muted while the radio is audible in the car;
on exit, its previous mute state is restored.
The playlist contains 27 original EA Trax songs with artist and title names.
The mapping is verified against the original data and audio checksums.
Other container streams no longer appear as songs on the radio.
Faulty tracks are skipped. If music is unavailable, the car remains usable.

LIGHTS AND NEON
F9 → Lights: headlight intensity from 0 to 300 percent and 14 color presets.
Stock, Xenon 4000K, Red, Blue, Green, Violet, Yellow, Extra Bright White, Xenon 8000K,
and extra-bright color variants. Selection and intensity are saved.
“Reset headlights” restores Stock and 100 percent.
The setting colors the light beams and front lamp surfaces together;
taillights, brake lights, reverse lights, and license plate lighting remain independent.
Preset names come from NFSU2; the lighting is tuned for Valheim.
On entering, front, rear, and license plate lights turn on; on exit, they turn off.
The light switch in the menu applies until the next entry. Brake lights also respond
when headlights are off. The plate uses the fixed original
UNDRGND-2 texture on its own small plate surface; its position is
approximate. Custom NFS license plate text has not been verified.
The white reverse lights are in the rear bumper. S first brakes while
moving forward; only near a stop does it shift into reverse and
turn on the white light. It stays on while rolling/braking in R,
until forward is selected or you exit.
The orange rear stripes are visible again, so far without a blinking function.
The tires are now 28 cm wide, while remaining 70 cm in diameter.
STYLE03 sport brakes have red calipers and 38-cm discs; the discs
rotate with the wheel, while the calipers only follow steering.
Neon is off initially. In the menu: turn it on, choose a color, choose pulsing.
Color and mode are saved. Neon is active while driving and
stays on after exiting when keep-running is enabled.
The original 350Z_NEON mask is adapted to the ground. Pulse behavior and
Unity lighting are custom adjustments and need to be checked in-game.

ENGINE AND TURBO
When launching forward, torque is increased by 20 percent up to about 11 km/h;
the bonus tapers off by about 22 km/h. The first audible gear is slightly shorter.
In reverse, a transmission whine with rising pitch/volume is audible.
This sound is synthesized, not an original NFS recording.
Audible gear changes happen earlier, especially off-road.
Shift interruptions: 140 ms up / 120 ms down. The suspension was not
changed for this sound adjustment. Original NFS turbo recordings
build under load and release pressure when the throttle is released/shifting.
Sample roles and triggers are a documented approximation; Rachel's
exact performance stage and original behavior still need comparison.
The horn is synthesized. NFS physics and the NFS audio engine are not running.

BURNOUT
While stationary, hold W and S together: the front axle holds the vehicle,
the rear wheels spin. Tire noise and smoke are added.
Both rear wheels and at least one front wheel must have ground contact to start.
Smoke/squealing only begins when the tires actually spin.
Release S while holding W: drive off normally. Release W: brake again/
select reverse. Spacebar, menu, focus loss, and exiting end the burnout.
It also cancels if contact is lost or the vehicle slides too much.
Nitro and purge are disabled while holding W+S. Reverse lights have been dimmed since 0.5.0.

NITRO AND PURGE
While moving forward with throttle and ground contact, the left mouse button provides thrust
and green exhaust flames. While stationary, gas jets instead come
from the bumper and hood. Original NFS sounds accompany both states.
A full tank lasts six seconds. After releasing, recharge while driving begins after two
seconds; a full charge takes ten seconds.
F9 → Nitro: turn on/off and enable optional flames when releasing the throttle.
This option is off by default and currently has no pop sound, because the
correct original sample has not yet been identified with certainty.
Menu, Alt-Tab, and exiting stop nitro. After the menu, release the mouse button
and press it again. There is no thrust in reverse or in the air.
Flame/gas animation, nozzle positions, and boost tuning are mod adjustments.
The rims now have a bright chrome lip and a gold-bronze center.

INSTALLATION
Close Valheim, then run python tools/install.py. The installer
backs up the previous files and verifies all copies with SHA-256.
Manual: copy all contents of artifacts/mod/ to
<Valheim>/BepInEx/plugins/NeedForValheim/, including radio/ and
audio/turbo/ and audio/effects/. The plugin ID and NeedForValheim.dll remain as before.
Settings: BepInEx/config/local.needforvalheim.prototype.cfg.

PLAYTEST
1. Open a local test world, spawn a vehicle, enter: lights and radio on.
2. F9: use radio, toggle lights, test neon/color/pulse, close menu.
3. Check brief acceleration, load changes, brake, reverse gear, and horn.
4. Compare driving modes on the same route; listen to shifts and turbo.
5. At night, test flat ground/slope: light beams, plate, neon without floating/flickering.
6. Check inventory, game menu, Alt-Tab, exit, and re-entry.
7. Remove vehicle: no lingering sounds/lights, player/camera controls free.
8. Hold left mouse button while stationary and driving: purge, green flames, tank.
9. Test nitro with menu, Alt-Tab, braking, reverse, and jumps.
10. Compare chrome lip/gold center by day and night.

Automated checks do not replace Unity physics/playback testing.
The external sound preview is not a recording from Valheim.
Error log: <Valheim>/BepInEx/LogOutput.log.
Audio sources: docs/AudioSources.txt; further tasks: docs/ROADMAP.md.


350Z INTERIOR (0.14.0, optional local import)
With the vehicle stopped, press F7: go to the left door, open it, visibly enter,
and close the door. Driving controls are then available. F7 while stationary exits
again; there must be room beside the driver's door. F8 also ends an
ongoing entry/exit and restores the player. Menus and Alt-Tab
pause the sequence. Please check the exact pose and clothing in-game.

DRIVER POSE AND WINDOWS (0.14.1)
F9 → Doors → Window tint: 0% clear to 100% dark, saved.
When seated, the left window is open: left arm on the window, right hand
on the steering wheel. To open the driver's door, the driver pulls the arm inward.
Doors can also be operated from the seat in the menu while stationary. The lockout
only applies during entry/exit; doors close automatically when driving off.

FLIGHT MODE (from 0.15.0)
In the car: F9 -> Vehicle -> Fly. The wheels fold horizontally; the car hovers.
W/S: forward/reverse. A/D: turn. Shift: ascend. Spacebar: descend.
With no input, it slows down and holds its position. Mouse remains camera control.
Four blue flames and a deep nitro sound accompany hovering without consuming the tank.
From 0.15.1: Shift up to 12 m/s upward, Spacebar up to 16 m/s downward; ground braking active.
W + left mouse button: flight nitro, up to about 158 km/h, six-second tank.
From 0.15.2, alternatively W + left Ctrl; after menus briefly release the key.
The status line shows NITRO ACTIVE, an empty tank, or the release lockout.
After releasing, wait two seconds, then recharge automatically while moving.
Rim visible side faces up; wheels tilt, flames point below opposite the thrust.
Additional analog speedometer beside the large NFS tachometer.
To land, choose Off-road/Street/Drift in F9. W/S and A/D correct the approach.
Exit only after landing and the wheels have unfolded. Flying cancels landing.
F8 also removes the vehicle in flight. The new flight mode still needs an in-game test.
